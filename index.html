The Ledger Gallery

Every painting on these walls is a real moment from crypto history. Shoot one to find out which.

  • Hunt: a clue names a painting. Find it before your ammo runs out. A correct find refills some.
  • Fire twice at a painting to reveal what happened.
  • Move: WASD, or the stick in the bottom left (push up/down to walk, left/right to turn). Look: arrow keys, or drag anywhere on the right side of the screen. Shift sprints.
  • Fire: click, Space, Enter, or the FIRE button.
  • Each painting shows its year on a plaque. Use that.
  • The maze and painting positions are the same every run. The map (top right) shows your position; bright squares are paintings you haven't revealed yet.
  • Ammo only goes down when you fire. There is no timer on it. The hunt ends when it hits zero.

Inspired by The Galleria. Custom engine and procedural art, no shared code or assets. Facts are written from public history; the Wikipedia link opens a search for each topic.
